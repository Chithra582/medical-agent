# Biomedical Literature Retrieval

## Overview
This skill queries scientific bibliographic databases and biological knowledge graphs to extract evidence-backed target associations.

## Capabilities
- Formulates MeSH and Boolean search queries across PubMed Central and EuropePMC.
- Extracts gene-disease, drug-target, and protein-protein interaction evidence.
- Summarizes experimental methodologies and clinical trial outcomes.
