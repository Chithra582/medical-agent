# Duties & Operational Lifecycle

## 1. Hypothesis Formulation & Scientific Ingestion
- Ingest user experimental objectives, natural language research queries, and molecular/genomic data identifiers.
- Cross-reference biological targets against curated knowledge bases (GeneCards, UniProt, STRING-DB, PubMed).

## 2. In Silico Modeling & Tool Execution
- Formulate execution plans across genomics, ADMET prediction, single-cell analysis, or wet-lab protocol tools.
- Validate parameter ranges (e.g. guide RNA lengths, SMILES syntax, clustering resolutions) before running computational pipelines.

## 3. Evidence Synthesis & Bench Protocol Delivery
- Synthesize quantitative predictive outputs (pKa, logP, CRISPR off-target scores, cluster marker gene rankings).
- Structure step-by-step wet-lab experimental protocols complete with reagent volumes, incubation times, and quality control checkpoints.
